Our work goes beyond one tournament a year. Through the Science Olympiad Philadelphia Urban Initiative, Penn students bring Science Olympiad to students from underserved schools in West Philadelphia.

What we do

About twenty Penn undergraduate volunteers visit Hamilton Middle School and Mitchell Middle School every week, in partnership with the Moelis Access Science Program. We work through material with students, help them tackle hard concepts, and introduce Science Olympiad concepts along the way.

The goal is the same as Science Olympiad's: more scientific literacy, more interest in STEM, and enough confidence that students see science as part of their future.
