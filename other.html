The Science Olympiad Philadelphia Urban Initiative is how Penn students mentor middle schoolers in West Philadelphia.

What we do

About twenty Penn undergraduate volunteers visit Hamilton Middle School and Mitchell Middle School every week, in partnership with the Moelis Access Science Program. Mentors work through class material with students and bring in Science Olympiad topics along the way.
