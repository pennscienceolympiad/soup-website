What goes into SOUP

Every February, Penn students who grew up on Science Olympiad write the tests, build the builds, and run the rooms for a Division C invitational. Coaches get to watch their teams instead of running an event.

  1. Register in the fallPennsylvania teams get a head start, then registration opens to everyone.
  2. Get the info document and waiversEvery competitor needs a signed liability waiver and photo release.
  3. Compete at Penn on February 13A full Saturday of events across Penn's campus, then awards.
  4. Take the tests homeResults go up afterward, along with tests and keys to study from.
