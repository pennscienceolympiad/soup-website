Science Olympiad at the University of Pennsylvania (SOUP) is a student organization that runs one of the most competitive college-hosted Science Olympiad invitationals in the country, and mentors Philadelphia students who might not otherwise get the chance to try Science Olympiad.

What we do

The Penn Invitational

Every February, Division C teams from across the country come to Penn for a day of tests, labs, and builds. We've hosted ten so far, from 42 teams in 2017 to 67 in 2026. Penn students who competed in Science Olympiad write and run every event.

Urban Initiative

About twenty Penn undergraduates visit Hamilton and Mitchell Middle Schools in West Philadelphia every week to mentor students in science, in partnership with the Moelis Access Science Program.

What is Science Olympiad?

Science Olympiad is a national nonprofit that runs regional, state, and national tournaments for about 7,800 teams of 15 students across all 50 states. Teams compete in events covering biology, earth and space science, chemistry, physics, and engineering:

  • Study events are exams on a topic, like anatomy and physiology or astronomy.
  • Lab events have students run experiments at the tournament.
  • Build events have teams design and build a device before the tournament, then test it against the rules on the day.

High school (Division C) and middle school (Division B) Science Olympiad pushed a lot of us toward science and engineering. Our goal is to give students in Philadelphia and around the country that same experience.
