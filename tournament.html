Getting here

Events run in buildings across Penn's campus in University City, West Philadelphia. Your team's rooms are in the information document. SEPTA's Market-Frankford Line stops at 34th St and 40th St, and 30th Street Station (Amtrak and regional rail) is a short walk away.

While you're in Philly

Cross the Schuylkill to the Philadelphia Museum of Art and run up the Rocky Steps, see City Hall and Logan Square, or head to Old City for the Liberty Bell, Elfreth's Alley, and ice cream at Franklin Fountain. University City has plenty of food within a few blocks of campus, from food trucks to sit-down restaurants.
