SOUP is run entirely by Penn students. We get partial funding from Penn's Student Activities Council, and sponsors and Penn departments cover the rest: tests and lab materials, build event equipment, medals, T-shirts, and keeping the fee low (and free for Philadelphia school district teams).

We'd love to build partnerships that help both of us reach more students.

What we're looking for

  • Introductions to guest speakers and industry specialists
  • Internship opportunities for Penn undergraduates and recent graduates
  • Help covering tournament-day costs like T-shirts and medals

What we can do for you

  • Your logo and a link on our website
  • A booth or panel at the tournament
  • Your logo on volunteer shirts or test cover pages
  • A shoutout at the awards ceremony and on our social media
  • A fundraising campaign together

Most of the students at our tournament are a year or two away from choosing a college and a major. Sponsoring departments can set up a table on tournament day to meet students between events and before the awards ceremony, when every competitor is in one place.

Any amount helps. Past departmental gifts of $500 to $1,500 have gone a long way toward making the tournament possible.
